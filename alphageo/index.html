Overview

What it is & why it’s complex

AlphaGeo is a location analytics platform designed to help real estate and infrastructure investors use data-driven insights to make informed decisions about climate risk and adaptation.

The complexity is in the data. Climate models, geospatial layers, and real-asset information arrive dense, technical, and multi-dimensional — and have to become something a non-specialist investor can read and act on.

System & Problem

Scoring a place, not a price

AlphaGeo layers climate projections, geographic data, and asset-level information onto specific locations — scoring how exposed a place is to climate risk over time, and how much local adaptation offsets it.

Behind a single risk number sits a stack of models and datasets. The challenge was making that depth legible and trustworthy to investors and infrastructure stakeholders who need a clear decision, not a science lesson.

  • Dense, multi-layered climate and geographic datasets — read by non-specialists.

  • Risk that has to feel rigorous and credible, not alarmist.

  • Different stakeholders — investors, infrastructure teams — with different questions.

  • Location intelligence and adaptation planning surfaced in one coherent view.

Product Translation

System → usable experience

I designed the full AlphaGeo platform — flows, maps, and data visualization that let an investor navigate climate risk without wading through raw datasets. The flagship Climate Resilience Suite leads with the decision: portfolio on a map, risk and resilience scored per hazard, financial impact quantified below.

Depth doesn’t stop at a single score. The Global Adaptation Layer breaks every hazard into its adaptation features — building strength, water storage, flood protections — scored from local district to national level.

And because investors ask where as often as what, Dynamism Signals maps market intelligence county by county — growth, risk, and opportunity readable in one view.

Website & Marketing Design

The front door to the platform

The public website is the first read investors get on the platform — it has to compress a deep technical stack into a single promise: see where risk is rising, and where opportunity is emerging.

Visual & Brand System

A language built for trust

The visual identity, website, and product UI share one professional system — giving a climate-intelligence platform the clarity and authority its investor audience expects. I authored the brand guidelines that hold it together: logo system, color, typography, and how the mark lives on imagery.

To keep that language alive beyond my own hands, I built AlphaGeo Studio — an AI tool where marketing and BD compose on-brand social visuals from templates, without needing a designer in the loop.
