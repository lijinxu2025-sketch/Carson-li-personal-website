A · Overview

A marketplace for the agent economy

Swarms Marketplace brings agents, prompts, tools, MCP servers, and skills into one product ecosystem. The design challenge is to make a fast-moving technical space feel understandable to buyers while giving creators a clear path to publish and monetize their work.

I shape the experience from product logic and discovery patterns to listing details, purchase flows, creator tools, and the visual system that holds everything together.

Audience
AI builders, teams, operators, and product creators
Experience
Discovery, evaluation, purchase, publishing, and monetization
Design goal
Turn a complex agent ecosystem into a clear, trusted marketplace

B · System & Problem

Many product types, one understandable system

The marketplace serves several product categories with different levels of technical complexity. Buyers need enough information to compare products confidently; creators need a publishing model that remains flexible without becoming overwhelming.

C · Discovery

Help buyers find the right tool fast

Discovery begins with a clear hierarchy: category, use case, capability, pricing, and proof. Search, filters, cards, and recommendation patterns are designed to reduce scanning time without hiding the technical depth users need.

Design decision

Show value before complexity.

Each product card leads with outcome and category, then reveals supporting metadata for deeper evaluation.

D · Product Flow

From listing to purchase without losing context

Product detail pages connect the story, capability, documentation, creator credibility, price, and access model. The purchase journey keeps those decisions visible so users understand what they are getting at every step.
