Overview

What it is & why it’s complex

Boros is a yield-trading platform on margin by Pendle. It enables trading of funding rates from various sources — including off-chain funding rates from centralized exchanges — and may expand to further yield categories.

Boros extends Pendle’s yield-trading thesis into leveraged yield markets. Margin, liquidation, and directional yield exposure compound the complexity: every one of those concepts has to be legible the moment a user opens a position.

System & Problem

Trading a rate, not a price

Funding rates are the periodic payments that keep perpetual markets tethered to spot. Boros turns those rates into something you can trade directly — going long or short the rate itself, with leverage. Because positions are margined, gains and losses are amplified, and a position can be liquidated if the rate moves against it.

That layering is what made it hard: leverage and liquidation risk stacked on an already-abstract idea. The experience had to keep users oriented while several fast-moving variables interact.

  • Funding rates as a tradable, directional instrument — unfamiliar to most users.

  • Margin and leverage that amplify both outcome and risk.

  • Liquidation thresholds that must be visible before, not after, they bite.

  • Long vs. short yield exposure and overall market structure.

Product Translation

System → usable experience

I designed the trading flows, screens, and data visualization so users can read their position, margin, and risk at a glance — and act with a clear sense of the downside.

Website & Marketing Design

A first impression that earns a first position

Beyond the app, I designed and shipped the Boros landing page — the page that turns a visitor’s first impression into an open position, and gives partners and press their reference point.

The same care extends to marketing: the cosmic serpent key visual I directed for launch carries through campaign art and social — one image system a viewer recognizes as Boros wherever it shows up.

Visual & Brand System

A sharper register for leveraged risk

The brand direction, app UI, landing page, and documentation hub share one coherent system — giving a leveraged trading product the clarity and credibility users need to commit capital.

So that sharper register survives every new market listing without me in the room, Boros gets its own canvas — the same design tool I built for Pendle, stocked with a Boros-style asset library so anyone on the team can compose visuals that stay on brand by default.
