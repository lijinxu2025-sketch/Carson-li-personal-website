The Project

Giving digital art a place

What is the real, and what is the virtual? INTERFACE answers with a building: a digital-art gallery for Singapore’s Orchard belt where immersive works are staged at architectural scale — a hologram stage, projection halls, an artist market, and a floating translucent cube whose skin mediates climate and image alike.

Beyond the spaces themselves, the project defines a business model for digital art — showcase, discovery, and commercialisation through low-latency networks — so the architecture and the economy of the work support each other.
