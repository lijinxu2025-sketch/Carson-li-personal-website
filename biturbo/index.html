A · Overview

A hybrid foundation for cross-chain DeFi

Biturbo combines Bitcoin’s security model with Ethereum’s liquidity and application ecosystem. The product challenge was to translate that hybrid architecture into a coherent experience for liquidity provision, stablecoin minting, and cross-chain services.

I led the product and visual design from early product logic through user flows, interface patterns, design system, and brand expression.

Audience
DeFi users, liquidity providers, builders, and ecosystem partners
Experience
Bridge, provide liquidity, mint stablecoins, and manage cross-chain assets
Design goal
Make a complex hybrid chain feel clear, credible, and actionable

B · System & Problem

Two ecosystems, one product language

Bitcoin and Ethereum users arrive with different mental models, expectations, and technical vocabulary. The system needed to explain how security, liquidity, assets, and cross-chain actions connect without forcing users to understand the infrastructure first.

C · Product Education

Explain the value before the mechanism

Product education focuses first on what users can achieve, then progressively reveals the hybrid architecture, network model, and transaction details. This keeps onboarding approachable while preserving transparency for advanced users.

PRODUCT EDUCATION · PLACEHOLDER

Design decision

Lead with outcomes, then reveal the protocol.

Each flow starts with the user’s goal and adds technical depth only where it supports a decision.

D · Core Product Flows

Make cross-chain actions feel continuous

Liquidity, minting, and cross-chain actions share a consistent transaction model. Network, asset, amount, fee, status, and risk information remain visible throughout the journey so users can act with confidence.

E · Ecosystem Experience

One view of assets, positions, and activity

The account experience brings balances, positions, rewards, transaction states, and network context into a single system. It helps users understand what they own and what is happening across chains without fragmenting the experience by protocol.

F · Product Translation

A reusable interface system for DeFi complexity

A modular design system standardizes asset selectors, network states, transaction reviews, risk messages, data cards, and feedback patterns. The same logic can scale from the initial product flows to future ecosystem services.
