The Series

When one room became a whole world

Loners in COVID documents the improvised interiors of isolation. Desks became offices, bedrooms became classrooms, and glowing screens became the main threshold to the outside world.

Rather than showing the pandemic at city scale, the series stays close to individual rooms and routines. Small differences in clutter, posture, light, and technology turn each interior into a portrait of its absent or solitary occupant.
