The Series

Space as an emotional atmosphere

Breathe explores the tension between enclosure and release. Monumental walls, narrow thresholds, and receding lines frame skies that feel physically present—soft, turbulent, and larger than the rooms containing them.

Each composition reduces the human figure to a small point of scale. The series moves between confinement and openness, using clouds as both weather and architecture: something that obscures, expands, and makes room for breath.
